## Quick Decision Shortcuts

| If the question... | Then it's probably... |
|--------------------|-----------------------|
| Asks "What is...?" or "List the..." | **L1 Remember** |
| Asks "Explain..." or "Describe how..." | **L2 Understand** |
| Gives a formula/method and says "Calculate/Solve..." | **L3 Apply** |
| Asks "Compare...", "Contrast...", or "How does X relate to Y?" | **L4 Analyze** |
| Asks "Evaluate...", "Justify...", "Is this effective?" | **L5 Evaluate** |
| Asks "Design...", "Propose...", "Create..." (something genuinely new) | **L6 Create** |

## The Two Critical Tests

### Test 1: The "Same Answer" Test (L1 vs. L2)
> Would every student write the exact same answer (word-for-word)?
> - **YES** → L1 (they're just recalling)
> - **NO** → L2 or higher (they're processing/transforming)

### Test 2: The "Relationship" Test (L2 vs. L4)
> Does the student need to examine how PARTS relate to each other?
> - **NO, just explain one thing** → L2
> - **YES, examine relationships between things** → L4
